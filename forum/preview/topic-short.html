The rule counts statements, not good intentions. You have six statements (the NOTE counts too) and one PLEASE. At least one in five must be polite, so you need two.

Make the READ OUT polite:

PLEASE READ OUT #1 WITH TEMPLATE "hello.html"

That puts you at two in six, exactly one-third, which is the most you're allowed. One more PLEASE and it's E099 PROGRAMMER IS OVERLY POLITE.
